Phase 02 — Strength & Power

Weeks 7–12

Six weeks converting raw strength into athletic power. Each week pairs a heavy strength session with a dedicated power session — Olympic lifting, plyometrics, and concentric-only work. Week 12 is a full testing week to measure exactly how far you've come.

Strength & Power

Convert raw strength into athletic explosiveness. Olympic lifting, plyometrics, and contrast training pair heavy strength work with high-speed power output. Ends with a full testing week so you can measure exactly what changed.

6 weeks
4 days/week
Commercial gym required
Solid strength base recommended

Full Body Press
Strength
Block A · 5 sets
6/4/1/1/1
BB Back Squat
▾

This is a ramping set — weight goes up as reps drop. Start at a weight that feels like 6 genuinely challenging reps, then add load each set as reps fall to 4, then 1, 1, 1. The final three singles should feel heavy but controlled — this is building toward true max strength, not grinding out ugly reps.

▾

Same ramping structure as the squat. Build weight across the sets as reps decrease. Take longer rests as the weight climbs — 3-4 minutes on the final two singles so you're pressing fresh, not fatigued.

02
Press / Power
Power
Block A · 3 sets
6 reps
BB Split Squat (concentric only)
▾

Start from the bottom position of a split squat and drive up explosively — there's no eccentric lowering phase, you step or drop back to reset each rep. This isolates the concentric power output without the fatigue of a full eccentric-concentric cycle. Focus purely on speed of the upward drive.

6 reps
Smith Machine Chest Press Throw
▾

Explosive press on the smith machine — push the bar as fast as possible so it briefly leaves your hands at the top, like a throw. Light load, maximum intent every rep. This trains rate of force development, not raw strength.

8 reps
Cable Woodchop
▾

Cable set high, chop diagonally down across your body, driven by rotating through your hips and trunk. Controlled return each rep.

6 reps
BB Step Up to Hip Flex
▾

Step up onto a box carrying a barbell, then drive your trailing knee up to hip height at the top — like a running knee drive under load. This trains single-leg power and hip flexion strength together, both critical for the sprint work coming in Phase 3.

6 reps
MB Squat to Throw
▾

Squat down holding a medicine ball, then explode up and throw the ball forward or overhead using the momentum from your legs. Full-body power transfer exercise — the ball should leave your hands with real force.

10 reps
Hanging Leg Raises
▾

Dead hang, raise straight legs to hip height using your abs. Control the lowering. Harder than knee raises due to the longer lever — drop to bent knees if straight legs cause swinging.

Block C · 3 sets
6 reps
Landmine Lateral Lunge
▾

Barbell anchored in a landmine, held at your chest. Step laterally into a side lunge holding the bar, then drive back to standing. Trains the frontal plane movement pattern that's often missing from standard programming but critical for change of direction.

15 reps
Weighted Push Up
▾

Standard push-up with a plate or weighted vest for added load. Full range — chest to just above the floor, full lockout at the top. Keep your body in a straight line throughout.

03
Full Body Pull
Strength
Block A · 4 sets
6/4/3/1
Trap Bar Deadlift
▾

Deadlift performed inside a hex/trap bar — more quad-dominant and more forgiving on your lower back than a conventional deadlift. Ramping set structure again: build weight as reps drop. Keep your chest tall and drive through the whole foot — the more upright torso position is the main technical difference from a conventional pull.

Pull / Power
Power
Block A · 3 sets
6 reps
BB Cleans
▾

Power clean — bar travels from the floor to your front rack in one explosive pull. Full triple extension through your ankles, knees, and hips at the top, then drop underneath to catch the bar on your shoulders. This is your primary Olympic lift for the next 6 weeks — technique matters more than load here. Start light and let the pattern earn heavier weight.

Oblique Crunches
▾

SL GHD Back Extension

Explosive BW Inverted Row
▾

Bodyweight inverted row performed with maximum intent — pull explosively so your chest briefly leaves the bar path at the top. This is the pulling equivalent of a plyo push-up, training upper body power.

Cable Pallof Press
▾

Anti-rotation press — cable at chest height, press straight out and hold briefly before returning. The resistance pulls you sideways; your job is to resist rotating. One of the best core stability exercises available.

20s
Cable Abduction ISO
▾

Hold your leg out to the side against the cable resistance for the full duration rather than doing reps. This isometric hold builds hip stability endurance that supports change of direction under fatigue.

8 reps
Zottman Curl
▾

Curl up with an underhand grip, rotate to an overhand grip at the top, then lower slowly with the overhand grip. Trains your biceps and forearm extensors together — useful for grip health with all the carrying and hanging work in this program.
